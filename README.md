Solmar Heat is a top-down 2D open-world crime game set in Solmar Bay, a procedurally seeded city of 12×12 blocks with roads, a park, a plaza, a beach, a pier, and the sea. On foot you can walk, sprint, punch, and shoot; steal and drive any of eight car models — from the Vela hatchback to the Falcon sports car — among 90 pedestrians, 26 traffic cars, and 18 parked cars. Crimes raise a five-star wanted level that brings the Solmar PD after you, and three story missions pay out cash: The Pickup ($500), Getaway Driver ($1,200), and The Bay Heist ($3,000), plus taxi fares and eight hidden cash stashes. Progress saves to your browser, the world is seeded (?seed=N replays the same city), and the whole game is plain static files with no libraries and no network requests — just open it in a desktop browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
